Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and hashed password.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic usage information: pages visited and actions taken within the app.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service. Specifically:

  • To display your expenses and spending summaries within your account.
  • To authenticate you when you sign in.
  • To improve the reliability and performance of the app.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

You may delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all associated expense data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts — to load the typefaces used in the interface. Google may log font requests per their own privacy policy.

We do not integrate with analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at:

support@spendly.app

We will respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
